## Natural Language Processing

GERA: a corpus of Russian school texts annotated for Grammatical Error Correction
<br/>
<i>Alexey Sorokin, Regina Nasyrova</i>

For their meticulous work on creating a new corpus for the task of grammatical error correction for the Russian language.


## Theoretical Machine Learning and Optimization

An asymptotically optimal algorithm for the minimum weight spanning tree with arbitrarily bounded diameter on random inputs
<br/>
<i>Edward Gimadi, Oxana Tsidulko</i>

The authors consider the classical problem of finding a minimum spanning tree. They propose a new search algorithm that significantly improves the theoretical performance bounds of the algorithm.

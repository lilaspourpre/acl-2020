<h2>Welcome!</h2>

The 13th International Conference — Analysis of Images, Social Networks and Texts — will take place offline from **Astana, Kazakhstan**. More specifically the event is organized with support of [Nazarbayev University](https://nu.edu.kz).

The world of Data Science changes every year. At AIST, we exchange our understanding of the Science state-of-the-art, as well as how it applies to life and business. AIST is regularly held since 2012.

The conference will be held offline. See [Venue](/venue/) page for more information.
